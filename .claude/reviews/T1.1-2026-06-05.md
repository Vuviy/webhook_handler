## Contract soundness (the core question)
- **3-state outcome is the right seam.** Distinguishing Invalid (401, forgery) from
  Error (5xx, PayPal API outage) is correct per FR-5 vs NFR-2 and matches ADR-0006 (A3).
- **Carrying eventId/eventType on Valid is correct** and avoids a second provider-specific
  parse in the controller (FR-11 + `NOT NULL event_type`).
- **Secret stays out of the interface** (injected into concrete verifiers via
  `ProviderSecrets`), so nothing secret can be logged through this seam — NFR-1 holds.
- **`provider(): string`** is enough for the T1.5 route→verifier registry.
- The only real gap is **invariant enforcement / edge-case wording** (findings 1–2);
  both are MINOR because they are tightened cheaply now and otherwise become T1.2/T1.5
  obligations.
